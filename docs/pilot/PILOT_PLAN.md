# Five-to-ten-person pilot — plan (not yet run)

**Status (2026-10-10): prepared, not started. No participants, sessions or
outcomes exist.** Nothing in this folder may be filled with invented or
model-generated observations.

## Question

Does a short practice loop — interview, quoted feedback, one targeted practice,
before/after comparison — give a person something specific and believable to
work on? Secondary: where do people get stuck, and is voice usable for them?

This is a usability pilot for a practice tool. It does not measure hiring
outcomes, validity of scores, or anyone's ability.

## Participants

- 5–10 adults preparing for a real interview, recruited by the team from their
  own networks. Aim for at least 2 software and 2 sales; note profession, not
  identity.
- Informed consent before the session (template below). People can stop at any
  time and ask for their data to be deleted (the app's *Delete my data*).
- Use a fresh guest identity per participant. Do not collect names in the app;
  the observation sheet uses a participant code (P01…P10).

## Session script (about 35 minutes)

1. (2 min) Consent, explain it is practice, scores are experimental.
2. (5 min) Setup: participant adds their own resume or background. Observe
   source review: do they correct or exclude statements?
3. (12 min) One round (or a full interview if time allows), Speak or Type —
   their choice. Note any voice or recovery problems.
4. (6 min) Feedback: ask them to read the top priority aloud and say whether the
   quote is theirs and whether the point is fair.
5. (6 min) One targeted practice attempt on that gap; read the comparison.
6. (4 min) Debrief questions (below).

## Debrief questions

1. Was the quoted part of your answer really what you meant? (yes / partly / no)
2. Was the main feedback point fair? Why?
3. What, specifically, would you do differently next time?
4. Did the before/after comparison match how you felt the second attempt went?
5. Anything confusing, worrying or missing? (privacy, scores, voice)

## What to record

Use `observation_sheet.csv`, one row per participant, filled during or right
after the session by the observer. Record what happened, not interpretations.
Leave cells empty rather than guessing.

## Consent text (adapt and have the team approve)

> This is a test of a practice tool, not an assessment. Your resume text and
> answers are processed by Groq (text) and, if you speak, Deepgram (speech),
> under their terms. We will take notes on how you use the tool, without your
> name. You can stop at any time and delete your data from the app. Scores are
> experimental and are not a judgement of you.

## Reporting

Report the number of participants, what was observed, and direct quotes only
with permission. With 5–10 people, report counts ("4 of 7"), not percentages
or statistical claims.
